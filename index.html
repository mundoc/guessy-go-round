How to Play

Setup

  1. Enter names for both teams and choose how many points are needed to win.
  2. Pick which word categories you want in the game.
  3. Sit in a circle alternating teams — Team A, Team B, Team A, Team B, and so on.
  4. If there's an odd number of players, one person plays as the Joker: they guess for both teams and never hold the phone.

Each Round

  1. One player holds the phone — only they can see the word on screen.
  2. Give clues to your team without saying the word itself.
  3. You can tap Change Word as many times as you want — even mid-description if your team isn't getting it.
  4. The timer runs secretly — it goes off at a random time!
  5. Once your team guesses the word, pass the phone to the next person clockwise.

Scoring

  1. After each round, use the + / − buttons to update the scores.
  2. First team to reach the points goal wins! 🏆

How to Play

Setup

  1. One person is the host and holds the phone. Everyone else splits into two teams.
  2. Every question was "asked to 100 people" from a group, like 100 married women or 100 teenagers. The board holds the most popular answers. Only the host sees it.
  3. The answers and points are our own best guesses at what 100 people would say, not a real poll.

Each Round

  1. Face-off: one player from each team answers. The host taps the answer they gave, or ✗ if it isn't on the board, then picks which team plays.
  2. The playing team takes turns answering. Each right answer adds its points to the bank. Each wrong answer is a strike.
  3. Find every answer to win the bank. After 3 strikes, the other team gets one answer to steal it all.
  4. Round 3 is worth double and round 4 triple. Made a mistake? Tap ↶ to undo.

Winning

  1. First team to 300 points wins. If nobody has 300 after round 4, play sudden death: one answer per team, and the higher answer on the board wins.
  2. Fast Round: the winners pick two players. Player 1 gets 23 seconds for 5 questions. Then player 2 gets 28 seconds for the same questions and can't repeat player 1's answers. Reach 200 points together to win the Fast Round. The host taps each answer as it's said, and can fix any answer before locking them in.
